Pakistan AI Governance & Data Privacy Matrix

How Guard AI provides an automated zero-trust compliance shield for Pakistani public sector institutions, enterprises, and financial systems operating under national data protection frameworks.

Statutory Law

PECA Act (2016 / 2025)

The Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act (PECA) strictly penalizes unauthorized access, identity fraud, and illegal interception of personal citizen data.

How Guard AI Enforces It:

  • Intercepts and redacts Pakistani CNIC numbers, mobile digits, and personal identity metrics before cloud dispatch.
  • Prevents identity fraud by sanitizing prompt contexts sent to external LLM vendors.
Data Privacy (Pakistan GDPR)

Personal Data Protection Bill

Modeled closely after GDPR, the PDPB mandates strict data protection consent, cross-border data transfer controls, and safeguards against automated profiling risks.

How Guard AI Enforces It:

  • Enforces real-time PII masking to fulfill non-disclosure and cross-border transfer requirements.
  • Enforces user consent validation and dynamic boundary context isolation.
National Policy

National AI Policy 2025

The Ministry of IT & Telecom framework requiring ethical AI standards, verifiable auditability, and national data sovereignty for public service integrations.

How Guard AI Enforces It:

  • Generates SHA-256 cryptographic audit logs for complete lifecycle traceability.
  • Deploys on local sovereign clouds (e.g., NTC / Pakistani Data Centers) to guarantee 100% data residency.
