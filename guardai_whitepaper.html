Guard AI: Sovereign AI Governance & Zero-Trust Middleware

Document Type: Enterprise Technical & Compliance White Paper
Target Standards: EU AI Act, GDPR, Pakistan PECA (2016/2025), PDPB, National AI Policy 2025
Author: Guard AI Engineering Team
Executive Summary: Guard AI is a zero-trust proxy middleware engineered to operate at the boundary of enterprise applications. Operating at sub-15ms processing latency, it redacts PII, blocks adversarial prompt injections, guarantees sovereign data residency, and maintains SHA-256 hash-chained immutable audit logs.

2. Technical Architecture & Verification

Guard AI executes as an asynchronous, high-throughput gateway with sub-15ms latency overhead, featuring 3 core layers:

  • Ingress Injection Filtering: Real-time vector classification blocking jailbreaks and command overrides.
  • PII Anonymization Engine: Regex + NER tokenizers scrubbing CNIC, IBAN, Passports, and PHI.
  • SHA-256 Ledger: Hash-chained immutable logging for cryptographic compliance tracking.

3. Sovereign Deployment Options

Supports Local Docker/Kubernetes containerization, NTC sovereign cloud hosting, and completely air-gapped hybrid deployments.
